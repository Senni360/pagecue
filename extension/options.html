PageCue settings

Runs entirely in Chrome. No companion, pairing token, or terminal is needed. Requests go directly to your chosen providers.

Keys and data

Keys are saved in this browser profile, restricted to trusted extension pages, and never sent to page scripts or synced. Browser storage is not an encrypted secrets vault. Blank key fields keep saved keys; use Remove to forget one. Keys from the old Python companion must be entered here once.

Selected audio, questions, and cropped screenshots go to your selected providers. When Jev is selected, its extraction provider receives the question/crop, and Jev receives the structured question and transcript. Audio files and history stay in this browser profile until removed with the extension or browser data. No automatic history deletion.

Keyboard controls

Hold S + T: new audio and question. Hold Q + A: new question with active transcript. Hold R + A: reveal/hide latest answer. Escape: cancel or hide. Alt+Shift+T / Q also authorize screenshot access. Alt+Shift+A reveals an answer. Alt+Shift+R opens recovery.

Media picker: Up/Down or Tab chooses a player; Enter selects. Question rectangle: arrows move, Shift+arrows resize, Enter submits. Mouse selection remains available. Ordinary shortcuts do not fire inside text fields.

OpenCode supports Responses and Chat Completions routes here. Choose the matching format for your model. Jev supports single-choice decisions here; use another answer provider for open questions. Choose an image-capable extraction/answer model for screenshots.
